What is Wick?

Wick is a simple, clean trade journal for individual traders. The app does not connect to any broker or exchange and does not carry out any financial transactions. It is used purely to manually log your own trades and automatically calculate stats from them (win rate, average win, average loss, equity curve and more).

The app requires an account (email and password, or Google sign-in) so that every user's data stays private and visible only to them. Once signed in, you can keep several independent trading accounts, log trades with screenshots and notes, and review daily and monthly summaries.

Why does Wick ask for access to your Google account?

Signing in with Google is an optional way to create a Wick account, as an alternative to registering with an email and password. The app only asks for your email address and basic profile info (name, profile picture), the same minimal data requested by any app that offers Google sign-in.

This data is used solely to create and identify your account in Wick, so your trade journal is tied to you and visible only to you. Wick never asks for access to your email, contacts, Google Drive, or any other Google data or service beyond basic sign-in. More details in the Privacy Policy.
